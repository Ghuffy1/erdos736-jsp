## Prior mathematical work

Paul Erdős recorded Walter Taylor's conjecture in *On the combinatorial problems which I would most like to see solved*, Combinatorica **1** (1981), 25–42. The relevant paragraph appears on printed page 14 of the available author scan. The modern catalog identifies it as Erdős Problem 736. [Original scan](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1981-16.pdf), [problem page](https://www.erdosproblems.com/736)

Péter Komjáth and Saharon Shelah proved a consistent negative answer in *Finite subgraphs of uncountably chromatic graphs*, Journal of Graph Theory **49** (2005), 28–38. Their paper attributes the relevant Theorem 3 to Komjáth. This is prior credit for the consistency result. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.20060)

Chris Lambie-Hanson's *On the growth rate of chromatic numbers of finite subgraphs*, Advances in Mathematics **369** (2020), article 107176, supplies the preceding forest-layer and increasing odd-girth construction. His April 28, 2020 CMU Mathematical Logic Seminar slides, *Finite subgraphs of uncountable graphs*, include a hereditarily countable construction of a continuum-size host with these properties. That construction and those structural properties are credited to him. The submission makes no claim to have first constructed a host of that size. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2020.107176), [seminar slides](https://speakerdeck.com/clambiehanson/finite-subgraphs-of-uncountable-graphs)

The submission presents the explicit finite-age compactness deduction from that structure to a ZFC counterexample to the Taylor assertion. It gives a self-contained construction through countable closures and coordinate inequalities, with every required lemma formalized. Priority for the stated Taylor corollary remains a matter for public evidence and expert review.

The compactness method is in the tradition of N. G. de Bruijn and P. Erdős, *A colour problem for infinite graphs and a problem in the theory of relations*, Indagationes Mathematicae **13** (1951), 369–373.
